## 📖 File Summaries & Operational Roles

### 1. [`business-knowledge.md`](file:///g:/My%20Drive/01%20Scando%20Integrated%20Solutions/01.01%20Marketing/01.01%20Social%20Media/knowledge/business-knowledge.md)
* **Role**: **Institutional Ground Truth**.
* **Key Doctrines**:
  * **The Big-Bang Reality**: Comprehensive Waterfall Go-Live works for mature 10–20 year enterprises with documented processes; it fails in 3–10 year scaling SMEs due to lack of readiness.
  * **The Anti-Agile Rule**: ERP cannot be run on loose Agile sprints. Scando's methodology is **Staged Waterfall with Change Absorption**.
  * **The 4-Stage Cycle**: Readiness Uplift ➔ Scoped Waterfall ➔ Change Absorption Pause ➔ Roadmap Update.
  * **Profile C: Egyptian Family Businesses**: Governance by personal trust and loyalty (One-Man Show); digital transformation is a massive Cultural Transformation before it is a software change.
  * **Technical Offerings**: Core Odoo, native Flutter/SQLite mobile frontline layers, Zebra/Honeywell barcode scanning, ETA E-Invoicing & E-Receipt compliance, Odoo School.

### 2. [`tone-of-voice.md`](file:///g:/My%20Drive/01%20Scando%20Integrated%20Solutions/01.01%20Marketing/01.01%20Social%20Media/knowledge/tone-of-voice.md)
* **Role**: **Brand & Persona Linguistic Standard (v3.3)**.
* **Key Doctrines**:
  * **4-Dimensional Matrix**: Persona × Account × Platform × Topic.
  * **The English Island Rule**: Scando LinkedIn (`ACC-001`) is the **only** English channel. All personal accounts and other platforms must be 100% Arabic.
  * **Sameh El-Gohary Persona**: Authoritative founder voice. **Strict Zero Corporate Self-Promotion Rule**.
  * **Direct English Business Terms**: Write `Waterfall`, `Agile`, `Go-Live`, `Big-Bang`, `Sprints` directly in English letters; strict ban on phonetic Arabization (no *سبرنتات*, *ووترفول*, *أجايل*).
  * **The Baton-Passing Narrative Flow**: Smooth paragraph-to-paragraph transitions; no fatalistic failure words (*فشل / بتفشل*).
