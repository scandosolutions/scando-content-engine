## 1. Operating Principle: Post-by-Post Deep Craft

Writing is executed **strictly post-by-post** (`one post at a time`). We never write posts in bulk or rush through copy.

## 3. The 10 Anti-Slop Drafting Protocols

Every draft written in the `content` field must rigorously obey these 10 protocols:

### Protocol 1: Direct Problem Opening (Zero Rhetorical Negative Setups)
* **Never** open with throat-clearing pleasantries (*"In today's fast-paced digital era..."*) or negative rhetorical contrasts (*«مش بتتعثر عشان كذا.. بتتعثر عشان كذا»*).
* **Rule**: Line 1 must dive straight into the operational reality, bleeding friction, or core diagnosis from the very first word. State what *does* happen immediately.

### Protocol 2: Pure Thought Leadership (Zero Corporate Self-Promotion on Personal Accounts)
* On personal accounts (`ACC-008`/`ACC-009`), **NEVER pitch Scando, mention the company name, or say *«في سكاندو بنعمل كذا»***.
* **Rule**: Teach the objectively correct engineering methodology. The reader must feel they are learning pure strategic truth from an honest industry leader, not reading an advertorial.

### Protocol 3: Direct English Business Terms for Tech Personas (`PERS-002`, `PERS-006`)
* When addressing tech-forward executives and GCC directors, write recognized international business and engineering terms **directly in English letters** (`Waterfall`, `Agile`, `Go-Live`, `Big-Bang`, `Master Data`, `UAT`, `Scope`, `Sprints`).
* **Rule**: **Never phonetically Arabize them** (❌ *سبرنتات*, ❌ *ووترفول*, ❌ *أجايل*, ❌ *بيج بانج*). Transliterating English terms into Arabic letters looks clumsy, unnatural, and amateurish to sophisticated executives.
* **Rule**: **Never duplicate or bracket them** (❌ *«ووترفول (Waterfall)»*). Integrate the clean English term directly into the Arabic sentence structure.

### Protocol 4: Single-Language Purity (No Redundant Parenthetical Translations)
* If you can say it in Arabic, say it in Arabic. If an industry term is universally known in English (e.g. ERP, Go-Live, Excel, WhatsApp, APIs), write it directly.
* **Rule**: **Never provide back-to-back parenthetical translations** (e.g. ❌ *«التسليم المرحلي (Incremental)»*, ❌ *«الانطلاق الشامل (Big Bang)»*).

### Protocol 5: Direct-Action Bullets (Zero Prefix Bloat)
* Never use synthetic bold prefix categories inside bullet points like `Category: Explanation` or `Subject: Text`.
* **Rule**: State the thought directly as a punchy, active sentence. Use typographic notation (`→`, `✓`, `•`, `1.`).

### Protocol 6: Radical Physical Specificity
* Every post must contain at least ONE tangible physical anchor: an exact operational setting (e.g. Tenth of Ramadan distribution warehouse, 45°C multi-branch retail hub), an authentic client quote (*«يا باشمهندس، أنا عندي مخزن واحد شغال بـ 45 شيت إكسيل»*), or verified numerical metrics (*"inventory shrinkage reduced from 14.2% to 0.38% in 60 days"*).

### Protocol 7: The Stance Mandate (No Neutral Hedging)
* Slop hedges everything (*"While X has advantages, Y must also be considered in an ever-evolving world"*).
* High-craft posts take an unapologetic expert stand: *"Big-Bang ERP without readiness is an ambush"*, *"ERP systems cannot be managed with Agile sprints"*. If everyone in the market agrees with the post, it is slop.

### Protocol 8: Authentic Cultural & Linguistic Texture
* Egyptian posts must use living Egyptian Business Arabic (*جرد، عجز، كاش محبوس، دفاتر ورق، مناديب*).
* GCC posts must use high-craft technical English and Gulf business registers.
* Never publish translated Silicon Valley marketing buzzwords.

### Protocol 9: Analytical Consultative Framing (Zero Fatalistic "Failure" Words)
* **Never** use fatalistic, accusatory, or melodramatic words like *«فشل / بتفشل / المشروعات الفاشلة»*. This language puts executive decision-makers on the defensive.
* **Rule**: Always frame operational and ERP challenges with analytical, consultative precision: *«ما بتوصلش للنتيجة المرجوة منها»*, *«ما بتحققش الأهداف المطلوبة»*, *«بتتعثر في تحقيق العائد الاستثماري»*. Speak as a calm, objective engineering advisor.

### Protocol 10: The Baton-Passing Narrative Flow (تسليم الراية وسلاسة التدفق)
* **The Baton-Passing Principle**: Every paragraph must logically hand over its conclusion to the opening line of the next paragraph. Never make abrupt cognitive leaps between attacking a problem and presenting a solution.
* **The 1-Idea Per Sentence Rule**: Avoid overloaded sentences with multiple nested conditional clauses. Break complex thoughts into crisp, single-clause sentences that the reader can breathe through naturally.
* **The 6-Stage Narrative Chain**:
  1. *The Tangible Action*: A recognizable operational decision on the ground.
  2. *The Natural Consequence*: What immediately happens as a result.
  3. *The Objective Contrast*: Distinguishing mature stable firms from fast-scaling firms.
  4. *Eliminating the False Alternative*: Explaining why quick-fix shortcuts (like Agile) don't fit ERP.
  5. *The Disciplined Engineering Solution*: The actionable, staged Waterfall cycle.
  6. *The Low-Friction Reflection*: An engaging closing question for executive discussion.
* **The Out-Loud Reading Test**: If you cannot read the entire post aloud smoothly without pausing awkwardly or running out of breath, the copy must be restructured.
