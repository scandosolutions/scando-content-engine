# Scando Social Media Operating Procedure: Harsh Post Review & Compliance Audit Rubric

**Document ID**: `SOP-POST-REVIEW-001`  
**Version**: `2.0`  
**Applies To**: Managing Editor, Brand Reviewer, Lead Strategist, Founder  
**Scope**: Auditing posts in `writing` before moving them to `scheduled`.

---

## 1. Operating Philosophy: The Harsh Gatekeeper

No post is moved to `scheduled` or published without passing this audit with a **100% score**. 

Reviewers are instructed to be **deliberately harsh, skeptical, and uncompromising**. If a post contains even one generic cliché, corporate buzzword, self-serving plug on a personal account, or AI translation tell, **it must be rejected immediately** for revision.

### 🚨 Gate 5: The 8-Test Anti-AI-Slop Diagnostic Screen

The reviewer must run the copy through these 8 strict screens:

1. **Test 5.1: The "Tapestry / Delve" Vocabulary Screen (Instant Disqualification)**:
   * **Banned English Tells**: ❌ *delve*, ❌ *tapestry*, ❌ *beacon*, ❌ *testament*, ❌ *unleash*, ❌ *game-changer*, ❌ *transformative landscape*, ❌ *holistic synergy*, ❌ *elevate your business*, ❌ *in conclusion*, ❌ *navigating the complexities*.
   * **Banned Arabic AI Tells**: ❌ *في المشهد الرقمي المتسارع*, ❌ *لا يخفى على أحد*, ❌ *تجدر الإشارة إلى*, ❌ *إطلاق العنان لإمكانياتك*, ❌ *الحلول الشاملة المتكاملة*, ❌ *في عالمنا المعاصر*.
2. **Test 5.2: The Emoji Soup Screen (Instant Disqualification)**:
   * [ ] **Zero Decorative Emojis**: The post must NOT open every bullet point with emojis (`🚀`, `💡`, `🎯`, `✨`, `🔥`, `👉`). Use clean typographic notation (`1.`, `2.`, `✓`, `→`, `•`).
3. **Test 5.3: The Single-Language Screen (No Parenthetical Translation Clutter)**:
   * [ ] **Zero Redundant Translations**: Reject any post that adds English translations in brackets next to clear Arabic terms (e.g. ❌ *«التسليم المرحلي (Incremental)»*).
4. **Test 5.4: The Direct-Action Bullet Screen (No Prefix Label Bloat)**:
   * [ ] **Zero Artificial Headers in Bullets**: Reject any bullet that starts with a synthetic bold category followed by a colon (e.g. ❌ *«تسليم تشغيلي كل 30 يوم: بنحل...»*). State the thought directly.
5. **Test 5.5: The Phonetic Arabization Screen (No Clunky Transliterations)**:
   * [ ] **Zero Phonetically Arabized Terms**: Reject writing English tech/business terms in Arabic letters for tech personas (❌ *سبرنتات*, ❌ *ووترفول*, ❌ *أجايل*, ❌ *بيج بانج*). Write recognized terms directly in English letters (`Sprints`, `Waterfall`, `Agile`, `Big-Bang`, `Go-Live`).
6. **Test 5.6: The Grounded Reality & Specificity Check**:
   * [ ] Does the copy cite real-world operational friction (e.g. specific warehouse pain, exact time frames, named technical layers)? If the post consists of abstract generalities ➔ **REJECT AS SLOP**.
7. **Test 5.7: The "Anyone Else" Inversion Test**:
   * [ ] Could any generic $5/hour software reseller or freelance agency copy-paste this post and put their logo on it without anyone noticing? If **YES** ➔ **REJECT AS SLOP**. The post must carry Scando's proprietary engineering methodology.
8. **Test 5.8: The Direct Opening Test (No Throat-Clearing & No Rhetorical Negative Setups)**:
   * [ ] Does Line 1 state the core subject, operational friction, or premise immediately? Reject rhetorical negative setups (*«مش بتتعثر عشان كذا.. بتتعثر عشان كذا»*) and polite throat-clearing intro lines. Start straight with the real problem.
9. **Test 5.9: The Fatalistic "Failure" Vocabulary Screen (Instant Disqualification)**:
   * [ ] **Zero Fatalistic Failure Language**: Reject any post that uses words like *«فشل / بتفشل / المشروعات الفاشلة»*. Operational friction must be diagnosed with analytical consultative precision: *«ما بتوصلش للنتيجة المرجوة منها»*, *«ما بتحققش الأهداف المطلوبة»*, *«بتتعثر في تحقيق العائد»*.
10. **Test 5.10: The Baton-Passing Flow Audit (Out-Loud Reading Screen)**:
    * [ ] **Smooth Narrative Transition**: Does each paragraph logically pick up from the preceding paragraph? Are sentences single-clause and readable in one breath? (Fail if there are overloaded multi-clause sentences or sudden unexplained leaps). Must pass the out-loud reading test without cognitive friction.

### 🚨 Gate 6: Formatting, Typography & Bidirectional Display
* [ ] Arabic text reads right-to-left naturally with proper spacing.
* [ ] Clean double line breaks (`\n\n`) separate short, readable paragraphs.
* [ ] Low-friction closing reflection question or clear call to action.
* [ ] Notes field includes concrete creative direction.

## 3. Scheduling Approval Sign-Off

When all 6 gates are marked **PASSED**:
1. Change post status from `"writing"` to `"scheduled"`.
2. Assign the approved `scheduled_date` (YYYY-MM-DD) based on the channel cadence matrix in [`POST_SELECTION_GUIDELINES.md`](file:///g:/My%20Drive/01%20Scando%20Integrated%20Solutions/01.01%20Marketing/01.01%20Social%20Media/data/posts/POST_SELECTION_GUIDELINES.md).
3. Record the audit sign-off in the post's `notes` field.
