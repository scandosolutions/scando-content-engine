# Scando Social Media Operating Procedure: Monthly Editorial Selection & Planning Guidelines

**Document ID**: `SOP-POST-SELECT-001`  
**Version**: `1.0`  
**Applies To**: Marketing Lead, Head of Growth, Social Media Strategist, Editorial Board  
**Scope**: Selecting and scheduling content from **Backlog** (`backlog`) into **This Month Calendar** (`this_month`).

---

## 1. Objective & Purpose

This standard operating procedure governs the selection of posts from the general **Backlog** into the active **This Month Calendar**. It guarantees that each monthly publishing cohort is not a random collection of posts, but a strategically balanced, multi-channel narrative engine that drives Scando’s commercial objectives, respects brand tone of voice, and maintains steady pipeline generation.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        BACKLOG                         │
│       All Curated Post Concepts, Angles & Hooks        │
└───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                            │
            Monthly Strategic Balancing Filter
          (Topics • Objectives • Channels • Sequencing)
                            │
                            ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  THIS MONTH CALENDAR                   │
│          Committed Monthly Publishing Cohort           │
└───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                            │
               Pushed one-by-one into:
                            ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       IN WRITING                       │
│        Active Drafting, Polishing & Harsh Audit        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. The 4 Golden Selection Rules

Before pulling any post from `backlog` to `this_month`, the selection batch must satisfy all 4 criteria:

### Rule 1: Revenue Objective Balancing (The "Why" Quota)
Every monthly cohort must distribute its weight across Scando's 5 core business objectives:

| Objective ID | Objective Title | Monthly Target Weight | Commercial Focus |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| `OBJ-001` | Adoption Engineering & ERP Rescue Positioning | **25% – 30%** | Inbound leads from failed ERPs & high-growth scale-ups |
| `OBJ-002` | Custom Software & Odoo UX Layers (GCC Export) | **20% – 25%** | High-throughput logistics & retail directors in KSA & UAE |
| `OBJ-003` | SME Core ERP Turnarounds & Inventory Control | **25% – 30%** | Egyptian domestic factory & distribution owners |
| `OBJ-004` | Transformation Proof, Risk Reversal & Case Studies | **10% – 15%** | De-risking decisions for late-stage prospects |
| `OBJ-005` | Ecosystem Leadership & Talent Pipeline | **5% – 10%** | Odoo School brand equity & technical talent sourcing |

### Rule 2: Topic Pillar Distribution (The "What" Quota)
No single topic may exceed 25% of a month's posts. Every month must prioritize **Egyptian Family Business & Cultural Transformation** alongside field engineering:

1. **Egyptian Family Business & Cultural Transformation** (`TOPIC-009`): **20%**
   * *High Strategic Weight*: Outweighs generic case studies. Focuses on the core Egyptian reality: managing by personal trust vs. systems, overcoming the One-Man Show bottleneck, and generational leadership succession (`PERS-001` ➔ `PERS-002`).
2. **Strategic & Contrarian Frameworks** (`TOPIC-001`, `TOPIC-002`): **20%**
   * *The Scando Method*: Staged Waterfall, change absorption pauses, and debunking Agile ERP myths.
3. **Operational Field Control** (`TOPIC-003` Warehousing, Barcodes, Inventory): **20%**
   * *Ground-floor reality*: Shrinkage reduction, physical counts, warehouse receipt habits, and frontline discipline.
4. **Advanced Tech Architecture** (`TOPIC-006` Flutter UX, Offline-First, APIs): **15%**
   * *GCC high-throughput export*: Native mobile scanning screens, Zebra/Honeywell integrations, and sub-second syncing.
5. **Financial Governance & Compliance** (`TOPIC-004` Cash Flow, `TOPIC-007` ETA E-Invoicing): **15%**
   * *Egyptian domestic protection*: Cash visibility, opening balance accuracy, ETA e-invoicing/receipt compliance.
6. **Functional Mastery & Career** (`TOPIC-008` Odoo School): **10%**
   * *Talent pipeline & authority*: Functional masterclasses and career development.

### Rule 4: Narrative Sequencing & Dependency Arcs
Posts must be ordered in logical cognitive sequence rather than arbitrarily:
* **The "Concept Before Deep-Dive" Arc**: Always publish the strategic diagnosis post first (e.g. `POST-001` on Big-Bang failure in scaling firms) before scheduling its tactical breakdown (e.g. `POST-025` defining what Readiness actually means).
* **The "Corporate Case Study Reflection" Arc**: When Scando Corporate (`ACC-001`) publishes an operational case study on a Tuesday, schedule Sameh's founder retrospective (`ACC-008`) on the same theme for Wednesday or Thursday.

## 3. Step-by-Step Monthly Selection Workflow

Follow these 5 steps at the start of each month (e.g. September 2026):

```
┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Step 1: Backlog Inventory │ ──► Audit all posts with status: "backlog"
└─────────────┬─────────────┘
              ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Step 2: Set Monthly Target│ ──► Determine total post count (e.g. 8 to 12 posts)
└─────────────┬─────────────┘
              ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Step 3: Run Balancing Grid│ ──► Verify Topic, Objective, and Persona distribution
└─────────────┬─────────────┘
              ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Step 4: Promote to Month  │ ──► Update post.status = "this_month"
└─────────────┬─────────────┘
              ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐
│ Step 5: Queue for Writing │ ──► Select Post #1, change to status: "writing"
└───────────────────────────┘
```

### Step 1: Backlog Audit
1. Open the Kanban board and filter by column `Backlog`.
2. Review post concepts against current real-world market trends (e.g. tax filing seasons, fiscal year ends, regional expos like Seamless or Leap).

### Step 2: Target Volume Definition
* For a standard month, commit to **8 to 12 high-craft posts** across all channels.
* Avoid content spam: 8 deeply researched, contrarian, high-impact posts outperform 30 generic AI-generated filler posts.

### Step 3: Run the Balancing Matrix Check
Verify that the proposed monthly selection includes:
- [ ] At least 2 posts addressing Egyptian Family Businesses (`TOPIC-009`), tackling governance by trust, the one-man-show bottleneck, and 1st-to-2nd generation succession (`PERS-001` / `PERS-002`).
- [ ] At least 2 posts addressing Saudi/GCC decision-makers (`PERS-006`).
- [ ] At least 3 posts addressing traditional Egyptian business owners (`PERS-001`) and financial controllers (`PERS-004`).
- [ ] At least 3 thought leadership posts from Sameh El-Gohary (`ACC-008`/`ACC-009`).
- [ ] At least 2 corporate case studies or field proof posts on Scando Official (`ACC-001`/`ACC-003`).

### Step 4: Status Promotion
For each selected post, update its JSON file:
```json
{
  "status": "this_month",
  "scheduled_date": ""
}
```
In the UI, these cards will move immediately into the **This Month Calendar** column.

### Step 5: Queue for Active Writing
Take the first post in the narrative sequence and change its status:
```json
{
  "status": "writing"
}
```
Now it is actively in the drafting and polishing chamber governed by [`POST_CREATION_GUIDELINES.md`](file:///g:/My%20Drive/01%20Scando%20Integrated%20Solutions/01.01%20Marketing/01.01%20Social%20Media/data/posts/POST_CREATION_GUIDELINES.md) and [`POST_REVIEW_GUIDELINES.md`](file:///g:/My%20Drive/01%20Scando%20Integrated%20Solutions/01.01%20Marketing/01.01%20Social%20Media/data/posts/POST_REVIEW_GUIDELINES.md).
