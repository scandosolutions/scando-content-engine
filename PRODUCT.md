# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small internal marketing team at Scando Integrated Solutions (currently solo or a very small team). They use this tool daily, sitting at a desk, in a focused work session, managing the full content pipeline across LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and Blog. Their primary job: move ideas forward through the pipeline (Idea → Researching → Writing → Scheduled → Published), track what's live on which platform, and plan upcoming content against strategic objectives.

They know the brand deeply. They don't need hand-holding — they need clarity, speed, and a tool that feels as professional as the work they're producing.

## Product Purpose

A content management board for Scando's social media and blog pipeline. It presents CSV-backed data as a scannable, filterable kanban-style view of content ideas, posts, objectives, and accounts. Success looks like: the user can assess the state of the entire content calendar at a glance, find any card by filtering on platform / status / objective / topic in under 3 seconds, and track published output per account without switching tools.

## Brand Personality

Expert, Approachable, Practical. The tool should feel like it was built by Scando, for Scando — not a generic SaaS product that happens to use the brand colors. Authority through structure and clarity, not complexity or decoration.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards: Notion, Airtable, Monday.com. These feel template-stamped, color-neutral, and interchangeable. This tool should be instantly recognizable as Scando-built.
- Over-designed agency portfolio aesthetics: flashy, motion-heavy, style-over-function. This is a working tool, not a showcase.
- Plain utility with no brand identity: Bootstrap defaults, unstyled tables, zero visual character. The Scando brand should be present and purposeful, not bolted on.

## Design Principles

1. **Pipeline at a glance.** The status of every idea — from raw concept to published post — must be readable in seconds. Color, typography, and layout serve the pipeline, not the other way around.
2. **Brand-first utility.** Scando Blue (#1c58dd) and Scando Green (#00f260) are load-bearing, not decorative. The tool should feel unmistakably Scando without a logo in sight.
3. **Signal, not noise.** Show platform, status, and objective on every card. Everything else is secondary. Avoid decorative elements that compete with status signals.
4. **Confident structure.** Montserrat's geometric weight communicates authority. Use it at the right sizes and weights to create clear hierarchy — headline, sub-label, body — with meaningful contrast between each step.
5. **Incremental by design.** The pipeline workflow (Idea → Published) is a core metaphor. The UI should make progress feel tangible and forward-moving, consistent with the Scando Method's philosophy of incremental value.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AAA (7:1 minimum contrast ratio for all text on backgrounds). The brand colors must be used at weights and sizes that maintain this standard. Platform and status indicators must not rely on color alone — pair color with text labels. No reduced-motion requirement specified; keep animations subtle and purposeful as a baseline.

- Secondary: Deep Blue #1677c4, Mint Green #00d455
- Hover/active state: #164fb8
- Neutrals: White #ffffff, Light Gray #f8f9fa, Medium Gray #6c757d, Dark #212529
- Primary typeface (English): Montserrat — Bold for headlines, SemiBold for nav/buttons, Regular for body
- Complementary typeface (English): Open Sans — long-form content, UI labels
- Primary typeface (Arabic): IBM Plex Sans Arabic — headlines and digital interfaces
- Complementary typeface (Arabic): Noto Naskh Arabic — formal / long-form
- Brand voice: Expert, Approachable, Practical, Confident, Empathetic (see knowledge/tone-of-voice.md)
- Content workflow: Idea → Researching → Writing → Scheduled → Published
- Platforms: LinkedIn (professional), Facebook (conversational), Instagram (human/visual), Blog (authoritative)
